## Recordkeeping rules

- Do not mark a contribution as released until the signed record has been received and checked.
- Record the actual legal rights holder, not merely the Git author or account name.
- If an employer, client, co-author, or assignee owns rights, record that entity and its authorization.
- Identify the exact commit, tag, archive hash, or file scope covered.
- Keep signed originals and identity/authority evidence securely. Do not publish personal data without consent.
- A contribution with unresolved ownership remains excluded and must be identified in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
