# lictest — Copyright Release + CC0 Template

A reusable starting point for projects whose rights holders intend to dedicate their own original code and documentation to the public domain as far as applicable law permits.

**This repository is a template, not legal advice and not a guarantee of worldwide public-domain status.** Do not apply it to third-party material or contributions until the relevant rights and authority have been checked.

## Intended model

1. Each rights holder signs a project-level or contribution-level release covering precisely identified material.
2. The rights holder separately associates CC0 1.0 Universal with the material they own.
3. The project keeps a durable record of the signer, authority, covered files/commit, date, and any exclusions.
4. Third-party material is inventoried and remains under its own terms unless its rights holder separately releases it.

The release form and CC0 serve complementary evidentiary and legal-tool roles. They do not create rights the signer does not own, transfer another party's rights, or override non-waivable rights under applicable law.

## Files

- `LICENSE` — canonical CC0 1.0 Universal legal code (English).
- `COPYRIGHT-RELEASE.md` — project rights-holder release / dedication record template.
- `CONTRIBUTOR-RELEASE.md` — per-contributor sign-off template.
- `AUTHORS.md` — rights-holder and release register.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` — exclusions and third-party component register.
- `docs/legal-model.md` — explanation of the model and its limits.
- `docs/signing-guide.md` — practical evidence and signing workflow.

## Before using this template

- Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`; delete inapplicable alternatives.
- Identify every copyright owner, employer, client, assignee, and other party that may own rights.
- Obtain an authorized signature from each relevant rights holder. A developer's personal signature is not enough if an employer or client owns the work.
- Define the covered material precisely (repository, paths, commit hashes, release archives, documentation, tests, generated files, etc.).
- Exclude dependencies, copied snippets, generated assets, trademarks, patents, and other material not owned by the signer unless separately cleared.
- Keep signed originals and proof of identity/authority privately and securely; publish only information the signer has agreed to make public.
- Have qualified counsel review the forms for the relevant jurisdictions before relying on them for a production project.

## Applying CC0

This repository's `LICENSE` contains the CC0 1.0 Universal legal code. Applying it to a project means the project rights holder is associating CC0 with the specific material they own. Merely copying this template into a repository does not release third-party material or establish that every contributor has validly applied CC0.

Canonical source: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>

## Suggested project notice

After completing the records, a project may use a notice such as:

> The project material identified in the completed rights-holder and contributor release records is dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal, to the extent permitted by applicable law. See LICENSE, COPYRIGHT-RELEASE.md, AUTHORS.md, and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. Excluded material retains its separately stated terms.

Do not use this notice until the releases are actually completed and the exclusions are accurate.
